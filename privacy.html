Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Noters is a mobile-first delivery app that helps you browse essentials, reorder favourites, and complete purchases in just a few taps. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Noters, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by your use of the app, and information from third parties who help us operate the service.

  • Account information - name, email address, phone number, and password when you create a Noters account.
  • Order and delivery information - items purchased, delivery address, order history, and any delivery instructions or preferences you provide.
  • Location data - with your permission, your device's precise or approximate location, used to show nearby items, estimate delivery times, and route couriers.
  • Payment information - processed by our third-party payment providers; we do not store full card numbers on our own servers.
  • Device and usage data - device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and how you interact with the app, collected automatically.
  • Communications - messages you send to support or through in-app chat with couriers, and any feedback or ratings you submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and process your orders.
  • To match orders with couriers and provide real-time delivery tracking.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To send order updates, receipts, and service notifications.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To improve the app, personalize your experience, and develop new features.
  • To send promotional communications, where permitted, which you can opt out of at any time.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to operate Noters:

  • Couriers and delivery partners - your name, delivery address, and order details, so your order can be delivered.
  • Payment processors - to complete transactions securely.
  • Service providers - vendors who help us with hosting, analytics, customer support, and communications, under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal and safety - when required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Noters, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers - in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy or a successor policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies on our website and app to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how Noters is used. You can control cookies through your browser or device settings; disabling them may limit some features.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account as described below.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access, correct, or update your account information at any time in the app.
  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw location permissions at any time through your device settings (this may limit some features).
  • Opt out of marketing communications using the unsubscribe link or in-app settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law. Contact us using the details below to exercise any of these rights.

7. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Noters is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we put appropriate safeguards in place to protect your information when it is transferred internationally.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by other reasonable means before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at privacy@noters.app.
